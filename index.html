About Riz Hotel

Where luxury meets comfort

Welcome to Riz Hotel, a premier destination for travelers seeking exceptional service and unforgettable experiences. Located in the heart of the city, our hotel offers a perfect blend of modern luxury and timeless elegance.

Since opening our doors in 2010, we've been committed to providing our guests with the highest standards of hospitality. Our dedicated team ensures that every aspect of your stay is perfect, from the moment you arrive until your departure.

Whether you're visiting for business or pleasure, Riz Hotel offers the perfect setting for your stay, with thoughtfully designed spaces, exquisite dining options, and personalized service that anticipates your every need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Check-in time is at 3:00 PM, and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability and additional charges.

Do you offer airport transportation?+

Yes, we offer airport transportation services for our guests. Please contact our concierge team in advance to arrange this service. Additional charges may apply.

Is parking available at the hotel?+

We offer valet parking services for our guests at a daily rate. Limited self-parking options are also available nearby. Please inquire with our front desk for current parking rates.

Are pets allowed at Riz Hotel?+

We welcome pets in designated pet-friendly rooms with prior arrangement. A pet fee applies, and certain restrictions may apply based on size and breed. Please contact us for details.

What dining options are available?+

Riz Hotel features three dining venues: our signature fine dining restaurant, a casual all-day bistro, and a rooftop lounge. Room service is available 24 hours a day.
